Leaderboard

The Rating is an Elo-scale Bradley–Terry score from head-to-head results on identical items, with each category weighted equally and 500 as the field average. Overall is the mean of the category scores (0–100). Every score is measured automatically: code is executed against tests, and verdicts are checked against ground truth. Click a row for details.

Categories

Whiskers show 95% confidence intervals. When two models' whiskers overlap, the gap between them may be noise.

Speed & hardware

Each figure is the median of repeated runs. Each run starts with a random prefix, so prompt caching can't inflate prefill speed. VRAM is measured above the idle baseline.

Efficiency

This is the local stand-in for cost per win: GPU energy measured during the benchmark runs, divided by fully solved items. It's priced at /kWh (set with --kwh-price).

UI gallery

These are the actual pages each model generated, rendered in sandboxed frames. The checklist score is in the corner.
